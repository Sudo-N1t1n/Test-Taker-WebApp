🔄 How It Works

1
Student enters name & email on the home page and clicks Start Test.
2
A 45-minute timer starts. Students answer 25 MCQs on practical physics topics at their own pace.
3
On submission, answers are graded instantly and saved to Firebase Firestore in the cloud — visible to the admin in real-time.
4
The student sees their score, pass/fail result, and a full question-by-question answer breakdown immediately.
5
Mr. Nagar logs in to the Admin Dashboard (password protected) to view all submissions in real-time, check per-question analytics, and search individual results.
